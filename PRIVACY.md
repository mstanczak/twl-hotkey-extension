# Privacy Policy for TWL Hotkey Enabler

**Last Updated:** September 13, 2026

TWL Hotkey Enabler ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**TWL Hotkey Enabler does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).**

- **No Data Collection:** We do not collect browsing history, keystrokes, personal credentials, account details, or analytics.
- **No External Transmission:** The Extension does not communicate with any external servers, third-party analytics services, or remote databases. All functionality runs 100% locally on your device.

---

### 2. Permissions & How They Are Used
The Extension requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

- **Storage (`chrome.storage.sync`):**  
  Used solely to save your hotkey settings, prefix options, and formatting preferences locally across your logged-in Chrome profile. No personal data is stored.
- **Clipboard (`clipboardRead`):**  
  Used solely when you explicitly trigger the Enhanced Order Paste shortcut (`Ctrl+O` or `Cmd+O`) to read the copied order number, format it locally on your computer (e.g., stripping hyphens and adding the order prefix), and insert it into the active input field. Clipboard contents are never logged, stored, or transmitted.
- **Host Permissions (`https://*.inforcloudsuite.com/*`):**  
  Used solely to inject the content script into web-based TWL terminal pages to re-enable standard keyboard shortcuts (`Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+V`, `Ctrl+X`, `Ctrl+A`, `Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+O`).

---

### 3. Third-Party Sharing
Because the Extension does not collect any data, we do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user information with third parties.
